Customer Segmentation

K-Means Machine Learning Model. This is an unsupervised model: it has no target column to predict, so instead of scoring answers it discovers customer groups in customers.csv by finding K centroids that minimise the total squared distance of every customer to its nearest one. Every number on this page comes from a real stats::kmeans() fit — nothing is pre-written or hard-coded.
